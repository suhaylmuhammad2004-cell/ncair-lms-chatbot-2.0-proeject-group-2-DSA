=== NCAIR LMS & INTERNSHIP PROGRAM OFFICIAL GUIDELINES ===

1. ABOUT NCAIR & FACILITY LOCATION
The National Centre for Artificial Intelligence and Robotics (NCAIR) is a special purpose vehicle of NITDA under the Federal Ministry of Communications, Innovation, and Digital Economy.
- Physical Address: Plot 790, Alimoh-Abu Street, Behind VIO Yard, Wuye District, Abuja, Nigeria.
- Onboarding Location: Physical registration happens strictly at the 50-Seater Hall, e-Government Building (PSIN) right after orientation for new interns.
- Support Details: There is no external registration support link or phone line because onboarding is done physically on-site with facilitators in the PSIN hall.

2. COMPLETE COURSE CURRICULUM & PROGRESSION
The official courses offered across cohorts follow this strict order:
1. Python Beginners
2. Python Advanced
3. Data Science Beginners
4. Data Science Advanced
5. Product Design Beginners
6. Product Design Advanced
7. Product Development
8. Embedded Systems

3. INTERN PROGRAM RULES (SIWES VS. NYSC)
- SIWES Intern First Cohort: Must register for and complete Python Beginners AND Product Design (UI/UX) Beginners simultaneously.
- NYSC Interns: Select their courses and pick their specific CDS Day on the registration form. Selecting a CDS day automatically grants an official excuse from classes on that day.
- SIWES Logbook Signing: SIWES interns must physically present and sign their logbooks at NCAIR Headquarters every two (2) weeks.

4. ATTENDANCE & GRADING POLICIES
- Class Schedule: Classes run three (3) times a week, lasting approximately 2 to 3 hours per session depending on the cohort.
- Attendance Threshold: A minimum of 75% attendance is strictly required to pass any cohort.
- Retake Rule for Low Attendance: Even if an intern passes all tests, assessments, and final project presentations with distinction, failing to meet the 75% attendance threshold results in an automatic retake of that cohort.
- Course Completion: Passing both attendance and assessments unlocks the next course in the sequence under "My Learning -> Courses".

5. TECHNICAL & ONBOARDING TROUBLESHOOTING
- Registration Window: Registration is opened physically on the day and closes strictly at 5:00 PM (buttons show "Locked" outside this window).
- Missing Invitation Email: Check Spam, Junk, or Trash folders in Gmail. Move the email to the Inbox first before tapping "Complete Registration".
- Password Requirements: Must be at least 8 characters long, containing at least one number and one letter.
- Document Upload Limits: All uploaded ID documents or letters must be in PDF or PNG format and under 2MB in file size.

6. RETURNING INTERNS PROCEDURES
- Existing Accounts: Returning interns do NOT need to onboard again.
- Sign-In URL: Go directly to https://lms.ncair.nitda.gov.ng/intern/signin using their existing email and password.
- Failed Courses: View the failed course under "Courses" and tap "Re-register" to retake it.
