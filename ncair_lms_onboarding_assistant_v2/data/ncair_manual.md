## overview | Start here: where to go and what onboarding is
@type: info
@page: 1
@link: lms_home
Onboarding is a one-time process for interns who are completely new. Right after the orientation, walk from the auditorium to the 50-Seater Hall, e-Government Building (PSIN). Form a single straight line and wait your turn. The manual walks you through registering on the NCAIR LMS (lms.ncair.nitda.gov.ng) step by step, and you can finish your own setup in about 10 to 20 minutes.

## bring | What to bring with you
@type: info
@page: 1
Come prepared, because without these you may not be able to finish your registration on the spot. Bring: (1) your acceptance letter from NITDA HQ, as a physical copy, a soft copy, or a clear photo on your phone, any one is fine; (2) your NIN, the slip or the 11-digit number known by heart or written down; (3) a smartphone you can open your email on, with mobile data or internet turned on (no data means you cannot register); (4) a password you will remember, which you will create during sign-up and need every time you log in.

## rules | The golden rules
@type: rule
@page: 2
Check your Spam or Junk folder, because the registration email sometimes lands there and every student should look in Spam if the email is not in the inbox. Use a password you can remember, not something random you will forget, because you need it to log in every time. Do not leave the hall until you are fully done: no intern leaves until they have successfully onboarded and registered for their courses. Registration only opens on the day and closes at 5:00 PM; before the day and after 5 PM the course button shows "Locked" and you cannot register.

## program-differences | SIWES interns versus NYSC interns
@type: rule
@page: 2
SIWES interns: both courses are compulsory. You must register for Python Beginners and Product Design (UI/UX) Beginners. NYSC interns: both courses are optional, but you may register for both at the same time. NYSC interns also pick their CDS day on the registration form.

## big-picture | How onboarding works (overview of the flow)
@type: info
@page: 3
The flow has six stages: line up in the hall; staff register you; check your email; create your account; sign in; register for both courses. Finish your own setup in 10 to 20 minutes. The reference card at the end condenses this into 8 steps: find email, complete registration, fill form, create account, sign in, Menu then Courses, REGISTER Python, REGISTER Product Design.

## step-1 | Step 1: Get registered by our staff
@type: step
@step: 1
@page: 3
When it is your turn in the line, give the staff your details. They add you to the system on their end, and the moment they do, an invitation email is sent to you.

## step-2 | Step 2: Open your email and find our message
@type: step
@step: 2
@page: 4
Open your email app (Gmail) on your phone. Look for a message from "NCAIR LMS" with the subject "Complete your NCAIR LMS internship registration". Tap it to open. Cannot see it? Check your Spam, Junk or Trash folder. Every student should look there if it is not in the inbox.

## step-3 | Step 3: Tap "Complete registration"
@type: step
@step: 3
@page: 4
@link: lms_home
Inside the email, tap the green "Complete registration" button. It opens a page on lms.ncair.nitda.gov.ng where you will create your account. If the button will not open or will not tap, the email is in Spam or Trash: first move the email to your Inbox (or tap "Not spam"), then tap the button again.

## step-4 | Step 4: Fill in your details
@type: step
@step: 4
@page: 5
Top of the form: Email is already filled in for you, leave it as it is. NIN: type your 11-digit NIN and check that your first and last name below it are correct. Lower down the form: Phone number, type the number you use. Program, tap and choose SIWES or NYSC, whichever you are. Gender, choose your gender. Password and Confirm password: create a password you will remember, then type it again. It needs at least 8 characters, one letter and one number. NYSC interns, choose your CDS day: when you pick NYSC in the Program box, a new "CDS Day" box appears just below it. Tap it and choose your day, for example Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday or Friday.

## step-5 | Step 5: Agree, then create your account
@type: step
@step: 5
@page: 6
Make sure the Password checklist shows all green ticks: at least 8 characters, contains a number, contains a letter, matches confirmation. Tick the box "I confirm that I am the intended recipient". Then tap the green "Create account" button.

## step-6 | Step 6: You're set, go to sign in
@type: step
@step: 6
@page: 6
@link: sign_in
You will see "Welcome aboard, [your name]" and a message that your account is created. Tap the green "Go to Sign In" button.

## step-7 | Step 7: Sign in to your account
@type: step
@step: 7
@page: 7
@link: sign_in
Keep the "Email" tab selected and type your email. Type the password you just created. Tap "Sign In". This is the same page you will log in at every time, so save it: lms.ncair.nitda.gov.ng/intern/signin

## step-8 | Step 8: Open your courses
@type: step
@step: 8
@page: 7
@link: courses
You are now on your dashboard. Tap the menu icon at the top-left. In the menu that opens, tap "Courses".

## step-9 | Step 9: Register for Python Beginners
@type: step
@step: 9
@page: 8
On the "Python Beginners" card, tap the green "REGISTER" button. On registration day the button reads "REGISTER". Before the day, and after 5 PM, it shows "Locked" and cannot be tapped.

## step-10 | Step 10: Register for Product Design (UI/UX) Beginners
@type: step
@step: 10
@page: 9
Scroll to the "Product Design Beginners" card and tap its green "REGISTER" button. SIWES interns must register for both courses. NYSC interns may register for both at the same time. When both are done you are finished: your account is set up and your courses are registered, and you may now leave the hall. Welcome to the NCAIR Internship Program.

## problem-no-email | Problem: I didn't get the email
@type: problem
@page: 9
Check your Spam, Junk or Trash folder. Wait a minute and refresh. Still nothing? Tell a staff member.

## problem-button-wont-open | Problem: "Complete registration" won't open
@type: problem
@page: 9
The email is in Spam or Trash. Move it to your Inbox (mark it "Not spam") first, then tap the button again.

## problem-password-rejected | Problem: My password is rejected
@type: problem
@page: 9
Use 8 or more characters with at least one letter and one number. Both password boxes must match.

## problem-locked | Problem: The button says "Locked"
@type: problem
@page: 9
Registration only opens on the day and closes at 5 PM. If it is the day, refresh the page or ask staff.

## problem-slow-internet | Problem: My internet is slow
@type: problem
@page: 10
Make sure your data is on. Move nearer a window, or ask staff about Wi-Fi.

## problem-forgot-password | Problem: I forgot my password already
@type: problem
@page: 10
@link: sign_in
On the sign-in screen tap "Forgot password?", or ask a staff member to help.

## returning | Already onboarded before? Returning students
@type: info
@page: 10
@link: sign_in
You do not need to onboard again. Just log in with the email and password you created during onboarding, at lms.ncair.nitda.gov.ng/intern/signin. If you passed the cohort: open Courses. The next course has been unlocked for you, so tap its green "REGISTER" button to enrol and continue. If you did not pass a course: open Courses. On the course you failed you will see a "Re-register" option, tap it to enrol again and retake it.

## quick-reference | Quick reference card
@type: info
@page: 10
Bring: acceptance letter, NIN, phone, mobile data, a password you will remember. Remember: check Spam, do not leave until done, registration closes 5 PM, SIWES means both courses are compulsory. The 8 steps: find email and complete registration; fill form; create account and sign in; Menu then Courses; REGISTER Python and REGISTER Product Design. Returning: sign in at lms.ncair.nitda.gov.ng/intern/signin; passed means next course, failed means re-register. Need help on the day? Ask any NCAIR staff in the hall.
